Try it (example parcels; the geometry is simplified)
  1. Tap 52-47A. Tap 52-44 next to it: a plain parcel just swaps.
  2. On 52-47A: + Parcels, tap 52-42A and 52-61 (across the road), Done. It's now a built parcel, saved.
  3. Tap another parcel: nothing happens until you Close it (acres ▾).
  4. Split it, or Draw an extra strip onto it. Open ▾ to edit or remove each part.
  5. Close it: it stays on the map (amber) and under Saved parcels. Tap it to reopen.
  6. With nothing open, Draw a parcel for land with no record. Refresh: everything stays.
